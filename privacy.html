Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 12, 2026

Welcome to Peerform ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience when using our fitness social media application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create a Peerform account, we collect:

  • Email address - Used for account authentication and communication
  • Password - Encrypted and stored securely for account access
  • Username - Your unique identifier on the platform
  • First and last name - Displayed on your profile
  • Profile picture - Optional image you upload to personalize your account

1.2 Content You Create

We store content you voluntarily share on Peerform:

  • Workout posts - Photos and captions you share about your fitness activities
  • Achievement posts - Milestones and accomplishments you choose to share
  • Comments - Your interactions on other users' posts
  • Likes - Posts you've liked or reacted to

1.3 Social Data

We track your social connections to provide community features:

  • Users you follow and users who follow you
  • Workout statistics and activity data
  • Leaderboard participation
  • Mentions in posts

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide core services - Enable you to create an account, post content, and interact with others
  • Personalize your experience - Show you relevant content and suggestions
  • Track your progress - Calculate workout statistics, streaks, and achievements
  • Enable social features - Connect you with friends, display leaderboards, and facilitate interactions
  • Communicate with you - Send important account updates and notifications
  • Improve our services - Analyze usage patterns to enhance the app

3. How We Store Your Information

Secure Infrastructure: All user data is stored on Supabase, an enterprise-grade backend platform with industry-standard security measures including encryption at rest and in transit.
  • Passwords - Never stored in plain text; encrypted using industry-standard hashing algorithms
  • Images - Stored in secure cloud storage with access controls
  • Personal data - Protected in a secure database with limited access

4. Information Sharing

4.1 What We Share Publicly

The following information is visible to other Peerform users:

  • Your profile picture, name, and username
  • Posts, achievements, and comments you create
  • Your workout statistics and calendar (visible to followers)
  • Users you follow and your followers

4.2 What We Don't Share

We will NEVER:

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your email address publicly
  • Disclose your password to anyone
  • Share your data with advertisers

5. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access your data - View all information we have about you
  • Delete your data - Request complete account deletion
  • Modify your data - Update your profile information at any time
  • Control visibility - Choose what you share and with whom
  • Export your data - Request a copy of your information

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account:

  • Your profile and posts will be permanently removed
  • Your personal information will be deleted within 30 days
  • Some anonymized data may be retained for analytics purposes

7. Children's Privacy

Peerform is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
  • Sending you a notification through the app (for significant changes)

9. Third-Party Services

Peerform uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase - Database and authentication infrastructure
  • Cloud Storage - For storing uploaded images

These services have their own privacy policies governing how they handle data.

10. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit and at rest
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Access controls and monitoring

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We will respond to your inquiry within 30 days.
